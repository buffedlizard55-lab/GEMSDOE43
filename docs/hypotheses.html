Decision rule

Every candidate runs on the frozen four-colour, 20-km catalogue holdout at equal 40,000-pixel mass first. Preregistered arms must beat the reproduced H42 baseline mean and win ≥3/4 folds. Arms selected after exploration (round-2b) are judged on folds 1–3 only (3/3 + mean). Winners must also pass novelty, input, format, rights, and disclosure checks. The holdout cannot forecast organizer hidden truth.

Round 2b · promoted

G43-SUP01 — supervised gradient-boosting ranker

Passed gate

Layers & signature

All 19 competition bands in band order + LiDAR scarp evidence (0-imputed) + scarp-validity flag = 21 features. Histogram gradient boosting (200 trees max, lr 0.06, 63 leaves, balanced classes, early stopping), trained per fold on training-block cells only. No coordinates.

Off-catalogue rationale

Supervised ranking learns nonlinear band interactions (magnetic × gravity × strain × conductivity × scarp) that hand-built geometric means cannot express. Trained strictly on mapped faults, it generalizes the learned multi-physics signature to unmapped structures.

Prior-work difference

H42/CG01/MG01 are hand-built unsupervised surfaces; H43's classifier used a different withheld-strand instrument; the organizer reference is a U-Net on random patches. SUP01 is the first per-fold boosted ranker evaluated on G43's 20-km blocked protocol with fixed packing.

Expected improvement & cost

Registered as a backup after MCLP falsification: moderate-to-high expected value (supervised ceiling above hand-built), medium cost (CPU training ≈3 min/fold). No new external data.

Measured result

Mean DTI 0.262666 vs H42 0.250744 (+0.011922), wins folds 0–3. Corrected gate (folds 1–3): 3/3, mean 0.263590 vs 0.253568. Slot-eligible; live-UNSCRED.

External data & status

No new external data. Scarp feature carries the same owner-mirror caveat as the H42 baseline. Shipped as the submission after format + novelty audits passed.

Round 2 · rank 1 · tested

G43-MCLP01 — lazy-greedy maximum expected coverage

Failed gate

Method & signature

Church–ReVelle covering on any continuous [0,1] surface: maximize Σ p·max-kernel-coverage at |S|=40,000 with the official triangular 300-m kernel. Exact lazy greedy (Minoux 1978), top-300k candidates by initial gain, deterministic ties. (1−1/e) guarantee over the candidate set for covering the surface.

Off-catalogue rationale

Placement, not geology: fixed spacing wastes budget on broad surfaces and starves ridges. Optimal covering converts the same geology into more expected kernel credit per dot — a formal floor no spacing constant carries.

Prior-work difference

All G43 arms used fixed-separation packing. Siblings tried max-cover on other surfaces: G32's smooth-maxcov was quarantined defective; G28's H37-1 passed interleaved but tied LOSFO far-field. This test used 20-km blocks, an exact solver, and matched mass.

Measured result

Arm A (H42 surface): mean 0.211122, 0/4 wins. Arm B (MG01): 0.150107, 0/4. Seven demand variants (raw, squared, blurred, binary, calibrated) all trailed packing on fold-0 exploration too. Rejected with mechanism documented.

Why it lost

Optimizer's curse on a miscalibrated map: surface mass sits in mid-value flats while truth concentrates on sharp top-score ridges, so mass-covering spreads dots over flats. Packing exploits the rank tail. Lesson: placement is downstream of probability quality.

External data & status

No new data. Code, brute-force-verified tests, and the falsification receipt stay in the repo; a calibrated-surface + MCLP combination remains the principled untested future arm.

Round 2 · rank 2 · tested

G43-MG01 — magnetic triple-gradient conjunction (+ ensemble)

Failed gate

Layers & signature

Competition tmi_hg, |tmi_vg|, |∇rtp| at σ=2 px, 99th-percentile normalized, H42-style geometric mean (0.05 floor). Rewards locations strong in all three magnetic derivatives at once.

Off-catalogue rationale

Triple-derivative agreement suppresses shallow noise and survey artifacts, retaining weak/deep susceptibility contacts under cover that never got mapped.

Prior-work difference

H42 used tmi_hg magnitude alone; CG01 used RTP-vs-gravity vector alignment. No audited operator required triple magnetic-derivative conjunction.

Measured result

Arm C (MG01 + sep4 packing): mean 0.205756, 0/4 wins — trails H42. Arm E (50/50 SH/MG01 ensemble + packing): 0.241241, 0/3 clean folds. Magnetic-only signal cannot replace scarp on catalogue truth.

Round 1 · tested

G43-CG01 — magnetic–gravity cross-gradient concordance

Failed gate

Layers & signature

Competition RTP magnetic field (rtp) and isostatic gravity anomaly (iso_grav_anom). At σ=2, 4, 8 grid cells, calculate gradient magnitudes and vector directions. Reward sharp, co-located gradients with appreciable strength and parallel or antiparallel normals; take the pixelwise median across scales.

Off-catalogue rationale

A buried fault/contact may juxtapose units with both magnetic-susceptibility and density contrasts. Concordant potential-field boundaries could continue beneath basin fill where there is no mapped scarp. The score surface itself reads no label or catalogue pixels.

Prior-work difference

Not an Euler source-depth cloud, terrain/LiDAR-scarp ranker, magnetic “worming” or catalogue-trained classifier. It is an unsupervised operator coupling two potential fields.

Measured result

Equal-mass 40,000-pixel emission, 4-pixel spacing: mean DTI 0.211567 vs H42 0.250744 (−0.039177). Lost all four folds; not promoted.
