Rank 1 · tested

G43-CG01 — magnetic–gravity cross-gradient concordance

Failed gate

Layers & signature

Competition RTP magnetic field (rtp) and isostatic gravity anomaly (iso_grav_anom). At σ=2, 4, 8 grid cells, calculate gradient magnitudes and vector directions. Reward sharp, co-located gradients with appreciable strength and parallel or antiparallel normals; take the pixelwise median across scales.

Off-catalogue rationale

A buried fault/contact may juxtapose units with both magnetic-susceptibility and density contrasts. Concordant potential-field boundaries could continue beneath basin fill where there is no mapped scarp. The score surface itself reads no label or catalogue pixels.

Prior-work difference

Not an Euler source-depth cloud, terrain/LiDAR-scarp ranker, magnetic “worming” or catalogue-trained classifier. It is an unsupervised operator coupling two potential fields. Exact global novelty still requires raster-level comparison before release.

Expected improvement & cost

Registered expectation: moderate value per implementation cost; no new external layer. This was a hypothesis, not a score forecast. Cost: low; both fields are in the input multiband GeoTIFF.

Measured result

Equal-mass 40,000-pixel emission, 4-pixel spacing: mean DTI 0.211567; minimum fold 0.204019; maximum 0.228287. H42 same-arm comparator: mean 0.250744. CG01 lost all four folds and was not promoted.

External data & status

No new external data. The two competition inputs are owner-mirrored and not organizer-authenticated in this checkout. No TIF is offered; candidate generation stopped at the validation/no-go stage.
