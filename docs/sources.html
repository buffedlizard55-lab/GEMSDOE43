Requires registration for competition data access; requires one 100-m single-layer GeoTIFF; generative AI is allowed but must be disclosed in the narrative; scores differ across evaluation stages.

LIT-01 · peer reviewed

Gallardo & Meju (2004)

Cross-gradient structural coupling precedent. It supports operator plausibility, not an expected score increase for CG01.

LIT-04 · peer reviewed

Church & ReVelle (1974), MCLP

“The Maximal Covering Location Problem,” Papers in Regional Science 32:101–118. Framing for G43-MCLP01: fixed facilities, coverage radius, maximize covered demand.

LIT-05 · peer reviewed

Nemhauser–Wolsey–Fisher (1978)

Greedy (1−1/e) approximation for monotone submodular maximization under a cardinality constraint. Scope here: covering the surface, not hidden truth.

LIT-06 · peer reviewed

Minoux (1978), lazy greedy

Accelerated greedy via lazy marginal-gain evaluation. Implemented exactly in src/gemsdoe43/mclp.py and tested against enumeration optima.

GEMSDOE28 (H36-1/H37-1)

Packing-rung study and the H37-1 max-coverage arm that passed interleaved validation but tied LOSFO far-field — the precedent G43's MCLP result replicates.

P-07 · owner site

LIT-07 · peer reviewed
