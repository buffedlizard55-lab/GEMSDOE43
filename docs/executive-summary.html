What H33-2-B2's 0.2747 represents

It is a model-projected value reported by the owner, not an organizer-confirmed score. GEMSDOE32 PR #9 describes H33-2-B2 as a catalogue-distance removal arm. Its 40,199-pixel B=1 base was pruned again at a two-pixel catalogue buffer: 2,545 dots removed, 37,654 retained. The owner reports a four-fold calibrated mean of 0.2679205 (minimum 0.1784544) and a +0.0048695 margin over its local calibration base in all four folds.

The same evidence record labels the separate projected value as 0.2746732, rounded to 0.2747. That projection transfers a model calibrated from owner-reported live anchors (0.2600 and 0.2708) and includes estimated credit and a safety factor near 2.08. It is not the 0.2679205 fold mean and is not a new organizer leaderboard observation. The PR explicitly says the repository has no organizer score for its artifacts.

Official DrivenData staff separately clarified that known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked from evaluation. Removing a nearby unmasked halo can still alter false-positive mass, but the removal rule is an emission-geometry decision—not proof that nearby pixels are or are not faults. Submission guide

These steps become actionable only after one candidate passes the registered holdout and all release audits. There is no current file to upload.

1 · Confirm the gate

Reproduce the selected H42 control. At equal mass, require the candidate mean to be strictly higher and at least 3 of 4 folds to win. Then run the full test suite and inspect all mismatches.

2 · Audit a genuinely new raster

Compare full pixel arrays against prior same-grid rasters (hash, support, correlation and top-mass overlap). Do not copy or rename a prior artifact. Confirm no labels or sample predictions leaked into the score.

3 · Check portal format

One band; float32; EPSG:32611; 100-m grid; bounds and transform match; finite [0,1] values in-area; null/NaN outside. Hash the final bytes and preserve the audit receipt.

4 · Prepare the narrative

Explain geology, data sources and licenses, limits, validation, and score attribution. Explicitly disclose that generative AI assisted research/code/documentation, how it was used, and which claims/results were independently checked.

5 · Give the portal a short comment

Use a concise factual note that names the unique candidate, summarizes its physical signal and validation, and points to the narrative for AI-use disclosure. Do not cite local holdout DTI as an official score.

6 · Choose one final file

The official rules require choosing a single final submission for both prize rounds. Verify the current official deadline and submission allowance in the organizer's rules before any upload.
