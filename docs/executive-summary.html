What H33-2-B2's 0.2747 represents

It is a model-projected value reported by the owner, not an organizer-confirmed score. GEMSDOE32 PR #9 describes H33-2-B2 as a catalogue-distance removal arm. Its 40,199-pixel B=1 base was pruned again at a two-pixel catalogue buffer: 2,545 dots removed, 37,654 retained. The owner reports a four-fold calibrated mean of 0.2679205 (minimum 0.1784544) and a +0.0048695 margin over its local calibration base in all four folds.

The same evidence record labels the separate projected value as 0.2746732, rounded to 0.2747. That projection transfers a model calibrated from owner-reported live anchors (0.2600 and 0.2708) and includes estimated credit and a safety factor near 2.08. It is not the 0.2679205 fold mean and is not a new organizer leaderboard observation. The PR explicitly says the repository has no organizer score for its artifacts.

Flagged conflict (IR-REPORT-01): the owner brief lists H33-2-B2 at 0.2778 as scored, contradicting the repository's own UNSCORED label. Cite neither as organizer-confirmed until the official board is checked in a browser.

Official DrivenData staff separately clarified that known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked from evaluation. Removing a nearby unmasked halo can still alter false-positive mass, but the removal rule is an emission-geometry decision—not proof that nearby pixels are or are not faults.
