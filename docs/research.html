G43 locally reimplements the field normalizations, owner-mirrored scarp decoder, geometric-mean surfaces, deterministic greedy packing, and DTI. The clean H42 model arms and controls in the saved report match at the declared 1e-6 tolerance. An archived owner arm marked historical_h33_CONTAMINATED is intentionally excluded: its raster is unavailable and the source itself calls it contaminated.

H42 uses detrended-elevation slope, a 12-band LiDAR scarp evidence derivative, and horizontal magnetic-gradient data. The H42 scarp mirror is for baseline reproduction only. It was not used in the G43-CG01 candidate.

The owner report is evidence of a prior-project result, not an organizer score. The local reproduction verifies the geometry and implementation of that validation recipe on G43's owner-mirrored inputs; it cannot authenticate those inputs or confirm a DrivenData leaderboard score.

No-go

Frozen operator

Use only the competition RTP magnetic band and isostatic-gravity anomaly. At 2-, 4- and 8-cell Gaussian scales, compute gradient magnitudes and directions; normalize each strength at the footprint 99th percentile; calculate the geometric mean of magnitudes times the absolute direction cosine; aggregate by the median across scales. Stable greedy packing emits 40,000 cells at 4-cell spacing, with the same held-fold boundary and training-catalogue guards as H42.

The score surface did not read labels, the scarp layer, prior submission rasters, or leaderboard data. The exact recipe and gate were frozen in registry/experiment.json before candidate scores were inspected.

Owner-reported context

H33-2-B2: 0.2747 is a projection

GEMSDOE32 PR #9 describes H33-2-B2 as deleting 2,545 pixels within a two-pixel catalogue buffer from a 40,199-pixel owner-reported B=1 base, leaving 37,654 pixels. The owner reports a calibrated four-fold mean of 0.2679205 and a model-projected live value of 0.2746732 (rounded to 0.2747), derived from owner-reported live anchors. The PR states that there is no organizer score for those artifacts. The projection is neither the four-fold mean nor an organizer-confirmed leaderboard score.

Official DrivenData staff clarified that known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked, while nearby unmasked predictions still affect scoring. H33-2-B2 is an emission-pruning rule, not evidence that the removed cells are not faults. Read PR #9 and the staff scoring reply.

Metric implementation

Verified against brute force

The metric module transcribes the official distance-weighted Tversky equations. For each truth pixel, TPw is the maximum nearby prediction × triangular kernel; each prediction mass pays FPw = p × (1 − best kernel weight to truth); FNw = |G| − TPw. The kernel is max(1 − d/3, 0) on the 100-m grid, with α=0.2 and β=0.8.

Twelve offline tests pass, including 30 seeded random fractional rasters against a direct brute-force transcription, an explicit known-pixel exclusion test, 300-m boundary tests, packing spacing, and synthetic aligned-vs-orthogonal field edges. See tests/.

Known-mask nuance: organizer staff said known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked from real scoring. This local CV does not pass those pixels as `known`, because then its held-out catalogue positives would be removed from its own validation truth. This is deliberately a different instrument.
