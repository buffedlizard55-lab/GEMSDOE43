Negative result · preserved

Why MCLP placement lost

Falsified

G43 implemented Church–ReVelle maximum coverage exactly: the official triangular 300-m kernel as the coverage function, a top-300k candidate shortlist, and an exact lazy-greedy solver whose (1−1/e) guarantee holds over the candidate set for covering the surface. The solver is unit-tested against brute-force optima on tiny grids (greedy ≥ (1−1/e)·OPT on every randomized trial).

On the scored holdout it still lost everywhere: 0.211122 (H42 surface) and 0.150107 (MG01), zero fold wins. Seven fold-0 demand variants — raw, squared, floor-subtracted, σ∈{1, 1.85, 3} blurred, top-200k binary, and training-calibrated P(truth|score) — all trailed fixed packing too (best: calibrated, 0.2051 vs 0.2423). Mechanism: the hand-built surfaces are rank-informative but magnitude-miscalibrated (SH's top score decile has only ~2% training-truth rate; most surface mass sits in mid-value flats while truth concentrates on sharp ridges), so mass-covering spreads dots over flats while score-descending packing concentrates all 40k dots on the extreme tail where truth density peaks. The guarantee is honored — the surface is covered near-optimally — but the surface is the wrong objective. This independently replicates GEMSDOE28's H37-1 lesson on a different surface and validator. Lesson: placement is downstream of probability quality; a calibrated supervised surface + MCLP is the principled untested combination.

Full exploration log: amendment_round2b.md · strategy memo: mclp_strategy.md.

Decision theory

The 0.2·DTI credit bar prices every dot

From the official formula, each unit of emitted mass adds exactly 0.2 to the DTI denominator regardless of its credit — so a marginal dot raises the score iff its kernel credit exceeds 0.2·DTI (≈0.052 at DTI 0.26). This one inequality explains the whole dotted-file lineage: thinning 121k→60k→44k dots removed already-covered mass, and the catalogue-flank prunes deleted below-bar dots near the catalogue. Conditional on the owner-inferred hidden count (~12.7k truth pixels), reaching 0.32 at 40k mass needs +18% credit density per dot — only a better detector (or live-calibrated mass discipline) can supply it. Derivation and limits: mclp_strategy.md.

Owner-reported context

H33-2-B2: 0.2747 is a projection

GEMSDOE32 PR #9 describes H33-2-B2 as deleting 2,545 pixels within a two-pixel catalogue buffer from a 40,199-pixel owner-reported B=1 base, leaving 37,654 pixels. The owner reports a calibrated four-fold mean of 0.2679205 and a model-projected live value of 0.2746732 (rounded to 0.2747), derived from owner-reported live anchors. The PR states that there is no organizer score for those artifacts. The projection is neither the four-fold mean nor an organizer-confirmed leaderboard score. A conflicting owner-brief report of 0.2778 is flagged as IR-REPORT-01.

Official DrivenData staff clarified that known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked, while nearby unmasked predictions still affect scoring. H33-2-B2 is an emission-pruning rule, not evidence that the removed cells are not faults. Read PR #9 and the staff scoring reply.

Metric implementation

Verified against brute force

The metric module transcribes the official distance-weighted Tversky equations. For each truth pixel, TPw is the maximum nearby prediction × triangular kernel; each prediction mass pays FPw = p × (1 − best kernel weight to truth); FNw = |G| − TPw. The kernel is max(1 − d/3, 0) on the 100-m grid, with α=0.2 and β=0.8.

Twenty-nine offline tests pass, including 30 seeded random fractional rasters against a direct brute-force transcription, an explicit known-pixel exclusion test, 300-m boundary tests, packing spacing, synthetic aligned-vs-orthogonal field edges, MCLP kernel/greedy/(1−1/e) checks against enumeration optima, triple-gradient and HGB-helper equivalence tests, and site link/download consistency checks. See tests/.

Known-mask nuance: organizer staff said known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked from real scoring. This local CV does not pass those pixels as `known`, because then its held-out catalogue positives would be removed from its own validation truth. This is deliberately a different instrument.
