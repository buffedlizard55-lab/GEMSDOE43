# GEMSDOE43 — preregistered experiment slate (2026-10-06)

**Original status at freeze:** before any GEMSDOE43 candidate raster or holdout score was generated. The repository began as a one-line README. All three were hypotheses, not validated findings. At that initial freeze, H46-A was the only arm authorized for the first holdout run, and only after its source-data gate passed. H46-A has since failed its source gate; a later explicit amendment re-ranked H46-B and recorded that its separate USGS source gate passed. The H46-B holdout is still not run. No submission slot is authorized by this document or its amendments.

## Objective and decision rule

Test whether one new, source-supported geological signal can improve the local catalogue-transfer proxy over the defined incumbent, using identical spatial folds, prediction mass, spacing, footprint and DTI implementation. The proxy tests recovery of *published catalogue faults*, not the organizer's private set of new faults; it is not a leaderboard forecast.

**Incumbent:** H42 `SH_basin_strong|sep4.0|N40000`, reimplemented from raw competition features and the pinned USGS-3DEP-derived LiDAR scarp raster. The previously reported four-fold mean DTI is **0.250744** (folds 0.242272, 0.247658, 0.255021, 0.258025). Recompute it unchanged on the same run before comparing the candidate. This historical number is a prior benchmark, not a substitute for the same-run control.

**Locked spatial protocol:** `fold_id=((row//200)+2*(col//200))%4`; 200-pixel (20 km) four-colour blocks; for fold `f`, `held=(fold_id==f)&footprint`; `forbidden=EDT(~held)<=3`; `training=labels&~forbidden`; `evaluation=held&(EDT(held)>3)`; and `allowed=evaluation&(EDT(~training)>2)`. Candidate and incumbent surfaces must not read labels. Labels may be used only to define these folds, apply the predeclared two-pixel training-trace emission guard, and calculate DTI. Pack **40,000** predictions per fold with a four-pixel minimum separation, using the same deterministic packing and `scripts/core.py` DTI implementation for both arms.

**Promotion gate:** H46-A must exceed the same-run incumbent by at least **0.005 absolute mean DTI**, win in at least **three of four** folds, and have no fold more than **0.010 below** its incumbent fold. Also require a documented source-byte checksum, eligible sample/assay counts, usable in-footprint coverage, correct CRS transform, and passing format/independence tests. If any gate fails, do not spend a submission slot. Do not tune this candidate on these four folds; any changed method requires fresh confirmation regions.

## H46-A — frozen feature recipe

1. Source is the official USGS *Multielement Geochemical Dataset of Surficial Materials for the Northern Great Basin*, Open-File Report 2002-227: [`data.html`](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/0227/data.html), [`ngb.csv`](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/0227/ngb.csv), and [`metadata.html`](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/0227/metadata.html). The report describes 10,261 stream-sediment/soil samples across 17 quadrangles. It notes that study areas used different analytical methods/detection limits and that substituted values are flagged. The authors' regional study reports a precious-metal factor characterized by Sb, Ag, As, Pb, Au and Zn. These statements motivate, but do not validate, this detector.
2. Use the official `ID`, `STUDY`, `SAMPTYP`, `LONGITUDE`, `LATITUDE` fields. Retain only stream-sediment codes 50, 61, 100 and 102; do not mix in soils, playa, talus or unknown media. Transform the documented NAD27 sample coordinates to the competition grid CRS EPSG:32611. Preserve the source's stated ±55 m possible location uncertainty in all interpretation.
3. The locked assay fields are `Ag(part)_ppm`, `As(part)_ppm`, `Au_AA`, `Pb(part)_ppm`, `Sb(part)_ppm`, and `Zn(part)_ppm` (partial ICP for trace elements; graphite-furnace AA for Au). For each element, use only numeric, non-substituted/non-censored observations; a source `*` qualifier in the immediately following blank-name column excludes that assay. Never turn detection-limit substitutions into positive anomalies. Equal-weight the six tie-aware empirical percentile ranks within `STUDY` (not labels or geography) to form a sample pathfinder index; require at least three measured elements. Do not tune elements or ranks against holdout labels.
4. Rasterize sample index and sample count to the official 100 m grid. Estimate local index at a fixed 2 km Gaussian scale (`sigma=20` pixels) as smoothed index-sum divided by smoothed sample-count; subtract the neutral percentile 0.5, clamp below zero to zero, and scale by the footprint's 95th percentile (clip at one). Areas without geochemical support score zero. This scale is deliberately regional; a 100 m output grid does not imply 100 m geochemical resolution.
5. Rank TMI horizontal-gradient magnitude within the footprint to `[0,1]`. The fixed H46-A surface is `sqrt(geochemical_enrichment * tmi_hg_percentile)`. No fitted weights, label-derived distance, catalogue masking, post-hoc pixel edits or reused prior prediction raster are permitted.
6. Outside-footprint output cells must be null/nodata as allowed by the official format; inside cells must be finite float32 in `[0,1]`. Preserve the official transform, dimensions and EPSG:32611. A template may supply only grid/footprint metadata, never its sample prediction values.

**Predeclared data-quality stop:** do not run H46-A if the CSV is not retrieved from the official endpoint or an authenticated byte-for-byte mirror; its source terms do not permit the required challenge use/sharing; fewer than 1,000 valid stream-sediment samples intersect the footprint; fewer than 500 retained samples have at least three usable pathfinder elements; or the stated geochemical footprint is too sparse to support the fixed rasterization. Record the failed check; do not substitute another dataset silently.

## Prior-art boundaries and known caveats

Reviewed family-level prior art includes H33 multi-physics Hessian/lineament consensus; H39 GeoDAWN radiometric ratios and orientation/condition surfaces; H40 GDR well/spring geochemistry/geothermometry; H41/H42 basin-margin, LiDAR scarp, relief and magnetic surfaces; H44 Euler, pull-apart, Curie, drainage-knickpoint and wavelet/worm variants; and H45 parity/phase, relief-conditional, side-distribution and survey-periodicity tests. H46-A changes the evidence family to official regional stream-sediment geochemistry and explicitly excludes the GDR `dist_known_fault_px` leakage field. H46-B uses mapped polygon contacts rather than SGMC fault lines; H46-C uses persistent optical alteration rather than GeoDAWN radiometrics. Exact overlap checks and data-quality audits are still required.

H45-PHASE previously reported a positive catalogue-transfer proxy (mean 0.208521 versus 0.193084 in four folds), but its own receipt marks it **research-only / no slot** because it lacked independent off-catalogue labels and a comparable inherited incumbent. It is not the H46-A comparator. H44's generated raster reuses a 37,654-pixel H33 prior-art base and is ineligible as a new artifact. The claim that a particular artifact produced the public 0.2778 row remains unresolved.
