## Selected experiment

Proceed first with **G43-CG01** because its two required fields are in the competition training raster, it needs no new data, and its falsifiable operator is materially different from the audited Euler, ridge/scarp, continuation, and supervised-classifier families. It is a *candidate*, not a claimed winner. Freeze its scales, thresholds, and emission budget before the holdout run. Reproduce the H42 baseline and same-mass controls first. If the cross-gradient arm fails the promotion rule, keep its GeoTIFF as a clearly labeled research artifact, do not spend a weekly slot, and report the negative result.

## Scope and evidence limits

- The GEMS target is geological-fault prediction, not direct geothermal-vent detection. Faults are indicative structures; a raster score is not proof of a geothermal resource.
- The official competition page specifies distance-weighted Tversky scoring (α=0.2, β=0.8, triangular distance kernel with R=300 m) and a single-band float32 GeoTIFF on the UTM Zone 11N / 100-m grid, values in [0,1], null/NaN outside the bounds.
- Public prior-project web pages and their own receipts are evidence of what those projects reported, not organizer verification. The current checkout began empty, so no local baseline result existed at registration time.
- Candidate originality is an empirical file-level property. It must be checked against all available prior same-grid rasters (exact hash, pixel support, correlation, and top-mass overlap) before any artifact is called unique.

## G43-CG01 preregistration and holdout outcome

Before reading any G43-CG01 holdout score, the operator was frozen in `registry/experiment.json`: use the competition RTP magnetic and isostatic-gravity anomaly bands; mask-aware Gaussian smoothing at 2, 4 and 8 cells; 99th-percentile gradient-magnitude normalization; per-scale `sqrt(magnetic_strength * gravity_strength) * abs(direction_cosine)`; median across scales; stable greedy packing at 4-cell minimum separation and 40,000 emitted cells. The H42 fold geometry, held-edge guard, training-catalogue thinning and validation truth were unchanged. No scarp layer or label is read while constructing the candidate score surface.

The local reproduction matched all clean H42 owner-report arms/controls at the registered 1e-6 tolerance after excluding only the owner's `historical_h33_CONTAMINATED` arm (its raster is unavailable and the owner labels it contaminated). The selected H42 arm reproduced mean 0.250744, min 0.242272 and max 0.258025. G43-CG01 scored mean **0.211567** (min 0.204019, max 0.228287) and won **0/4 folds**. The gate failed. No candidate GeoTIFF was generated for download, no submission slot was used, and CG01 is not eligible for submission.

This is a negative result against withheld published catalogue faults, not an estimate of hidden organizer truth. The report and fold-level receipts are in `evidence/holdout_g43_cg01.json`; exact formulas are covered by the local brute-force metric tests. If another candidate is pursued, preregister it before scoring and compare it at equal mass to the reproduced H42 arm; do not revise CG01's gate post hoc.
